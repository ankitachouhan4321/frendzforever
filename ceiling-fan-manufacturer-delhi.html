Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your BLDC ceiling fan different?

Our BLDC ceiling fan uses advanced brushless motor technology for up to 60% less power while giving strong airflow and BEE 5-star rating.

What is the difference between normal and BLDC fans?

A normal fan uses about 75 watts. Our BLDC models use only 28–32 watts and run quietly on home inverters without buzzing.
