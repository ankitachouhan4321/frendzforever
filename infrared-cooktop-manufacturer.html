How Does an Infrared Cooktop Work?

It’s easier than you think.

Our infrared cooktop sends heat straight to the bottom of your cookware using infrared rays. No magnetic field like regular induction, so it works with every type of metal or glass utensil. The heat is fast, even, and gentle on food – perfect for slow-cooked dal or quick rotis.

Many customers ask us, “Is it better than normal induction?” Yes, for most Indian homes, because you don’t need to buy new cookware. Plus, it stays cool around the edges, so it’s safer for kids and elders.

Infrared vs. Induction: Which One Should You Buy?

Many people ask: "Is an infrared induction cooktop the same as a normal induction?" The answer is No.

While energy-efficient induction is good, it only works with magnetic steel. An electric infrared cooktop (also called a radiant cooktop) uses a high-tech heating coil under scratch-resistant crystal glass. It gets hot just like a gas stove, making it the best infrared cooktop in India for people who don't want to throw away their old pans.
