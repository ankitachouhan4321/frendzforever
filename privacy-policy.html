Privacy Policy

Introduction

At Frendz Forever, we value and respect your privacy and we are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, handle, use, and store your personal data/information when you use our website. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your personal data and how we will treat it. That’s why we always keep it private. To protect your personal information, we use a privacy policy.

Data Collection

When you use our website, we may collect the following types of personal data:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and postal address, which you provide to us when you create an account or sign up for our newsletter.
  • Location data, which we may collect when you use our website to explore offers for nearby offline stores.
  • Usage data, including information about how you use our website, such as the pages you visit, the offers you view, and the duration of your visits.
  • Technical data, including your internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, and version, time zone setting, browser plug-in types and versions, and operating system and platform.

Data Sharing

We may share your personal data with third-party service providers who help us operate our website, such as hosting providers, email service providers, and analytics providers. We may also share your personal data with our business partners and affiliates for marketing and advertising purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We store your personal data on secure servers and use industry-standard security technologies to protect your data while it is in transit.

Data Retention

We will retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required by law. When your personal data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, we will securely delete or anonymize it.
