Frequently Ask Questions

How does an induction cooktop save money?

It heats the metal of the pan directly instead of wasting heat in the air like gas. This makes it faster and lowers your monthly bills.

Is a 2000W induction cooktop enough for Indian cooking?

Yes. It handles deep frying, rotis, and heavy pressure cooking just like a high-flame gas burner.

Do your cooktops work with every utensil?

Our induction models need flat-bottom induction-compatible pans. If you want to use any utensil, ask us about our infrared options.
